Cookie & Local Storage Notice

This notice describes browser-side storage used by the public experience.

Public discovery

The current interface uses browser local storage for certain interface translation/cache values. The public discovery flow does not intentionally use browser cookies as a substitute for consent records or backend authorization.

Third-party services

When a user opens a third-party service such as a calendar, messaging, voice or document destination, that service may apply its own cookies or local storage. Those controls are governed by the third party.

Owner consoles

Authenticated owner consoles may use authentication session storage required to maintain a signed-in session. Sensitive business data is not authorized merely because a browser can load the page; backend authorization is required.

Managing storage

Users can manage or clear browser storage through their browser settings. Clearing required authentication storage may sign the user out or reset optional interface preferences.
